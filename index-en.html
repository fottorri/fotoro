About Us

Hello! FOTORO began shooting wedding snaps in Rome in May 2018 and is run by a single lead photographer. I know how unsettling it is to book a studio where the photographer can change without you knowing, or where the photos on show are of unknown origin and you can't tell who took them. That is why I, as the sole lead photographer, personally handle consultation, shooting and retouching. Three accounts are active (fotoro_, fottorri and fotoro.it), and every photo uploaded to them was taken by me.

Since 2023, after COVID, I have been based in Florence. Alongside Florence, FOTORO shoots in Tuscany, the Dolomites and all across Italy, and it is the only Korean studio in Italy that can offer the "Salon Banha Hair & Makeup + Dress Package" nationwide. Salon Banha, the makeup partner for the package, has worked not only in weddings but in many other fields, and their skill has already been proven by countless reviews.

The name FOTORO comes from combining Foto(grafia) and Roma. It started out as "fotoroma", but for fear of ending up shooting only in Rome, the "ma" was dropped and it became "fotoro". The "it" stands for Italia. There's no deeper meaning, really...

FOTORO's first priority is to leave you with photos that never grow tiresome or childish, however much time passes, through understated direction and unobtrusive color tones, and it pursues a classic and simple style. At the same time, so as never to lose sight of the essence of a travel photo, the goal is to capture your trip in a variety of places, compositions and moods.
