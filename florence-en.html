Good to Know

🍦

Gelato, café and Duomo-view accommodation shots can be included if you tell us in advance — we will divide the time accordingly.

Café and accommodation costs are at the client's expense — if you have booked a lovely place to stay, let us know ahead of time!

🗺️

The locations listed are general guidance, not the order in which we will shoot.

Depending on local conditions, some backdrops may be under construction or unavailable for shooting.

📦

Original files are delivered within 1-14 days of the shoot date.

🎨

Retouched photos are completed 21-90 days after your request.

Delivery within 2 weeks is available on request.

⏱️

Session durations are the total time, including travel and waiting.

For the hill transfer, choose walking or a taxi (a taxi is required for 2-hour sessions)

Taxi fare is about 20 EUR, paid by the client

👫

Package contents and pricing are based on a shoot for two people (an opposite-sex couple); each additional person is +50 EUR.

Prices apply with consent to SNS photo posting.

⛰️

Dawn and Sunrise sessions always start on the hill; Morning sessions can start on the hill or in the city (city is the default).

💶

The balance is due on the day of the shoot, after the shoot ends.

If you pay the balance via PayPal, a 5% PayPal fee is added.
