About Us

Hello! FOTORO began shooting wedding snaps in Rome back in May 2018 and is currently based in Florence. Alongside Florence, we shoot across Tuscany, the Dolomites, and all throughout Italy. We are also proud to be the only studio in Italy offering the nationwide "Salon Banha Hair & Makeup + Dress Package".

The name FOTORO comes from combining Foto(grafia) and Roma. It was originally "fotoroma," but we dropped the "ma" to keep our creative reach open beyond Rome, leaving us with "fotoro". The "it" simply stands for Italia.

Our main goal is to deliver timeless photos you will never tire of. Through natural direction and understated color tones, we create images that stay effortlessly beautiful over time, pursuing a truly classic and simple aesthetic above all.
